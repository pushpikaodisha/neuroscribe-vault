# Cognitive Architectures & Dual Process Theory

Cognitive architectures seek to formalize the invariant computational structures of the human mind. Pioneered by Allen Newell's plea for "unified theories of cognition," these frameworks model perception, attention, working memory, long-term semantic knowledge, and goal evaluation within an integrated computational harness.

## Kahneman's Dual Process Framework

Popularized by Daniel Kahneman and Amos Tversky, cognitive processes divide into two distinct modes of computation:

| Metric | System 1 (Intuitive / Fast) | System 2 (Deliberative / Slow) |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed** | Milliseconds, reflex-speed | Seconds to minutes |
| **Effort & Energy** | Low metabolic demand, automatic | High metabolic demand, conscious effort |
| **Awareness** | Inaccessible sub-symbolic execution | Full phenomenological reporting |
| **Capacity** | Massive parallel throughput | Strict serial bottleneck ($\sim 4$ chunks) |
| **Computational Mode** | Heuristic, associative vector retrieval | Rule-based, symbolic manipulation |
| **Synthetic Analog** | [[Neural Networks & Deep Learning]] | Search algorithms & symbolic [[Artificial Intelligence]] |

## Unified Computational Architectures

### ACT-R (Adaptive Control of Thought—Rational)
Developed by John R. Anderson, ACT-R models the interaction between:
1. **Declarative Memory**: Chunks of factual knowledge indexed by activation strength.
2. **Procedural Memory**: Production rules ($IF \dots THEN \dots$) executed via matching algorithms.
3. **Perceptual-Motor Buffers**: Interfaces that ground symbolic computations into embodied sensorimotor channels.

### SOAR (State, Operator And Result)
Developed by Laird, Newell, and Rosenbloom, SOAR structures cognition around universal subgoaling and chunking: whenever an impasse is reached in problem space, the architecture recursively spawns a sub-state to resolve ambiguity.

## Synergies and Foundations

- **Biological Substrates**: System 1 is mediated by subcortical amygdala, basal ganglia, and sensory cortices, whereas System 2 relies heavily on the prefrontal cortex and anterior cingulate, as explored in [[Neuroscience & Synaptic Plasticity]].
- **Predictive Optimization**: Both systems function harmoniously to resolve variational prediction errors, grounding their division in the [[Free Energy Principle & Active Inference]].
- **Conscious Gating**: System 2 corresponds closely to the conscious global broadcast highlighted by Global Workspace Theory in [[Consciousness & Philosophy of Mind]].
- **Hybrid AI Systems**: Modern neuro-symbolic AI seeks to combine the raw perceptual vector power of deep nets with the robust verifiability of symbolic planners, creating resilient agents for [[Complex Systems & Emergence]].
